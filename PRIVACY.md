# Privacy Policy for Kanji Kollect

**Last updated:** March 2025

## Overview

Kanji Kollect ("the app") is a Japanese learning application. This policy describes how the app handles your information.

## Data storage and collection

- **No account required.** The app does not collect or transmit your personal data to our servers or any third party.
- **Data stays on your device.** Content you create (saved items, photos, preferences) is stored locally on your device using the device’s storage and local database (SQLite). We do not have access to this data.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Permissions and how we use them

- **Camera** — Used only to capture photos of Japanese text for use within the app. Photos are stored on your device and are not sent to us or anyone else.
- **Photo library (read)** — Used only to let you choose existing images of Japanese text to import into the app. We do not upload or transmit these images.

## Data you delete

When you delete content in the app or uninstall the app, that data is removed from your device. We do not retain copies.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the app’s handling of data, you can open an issue in the app’s public repository or contact the developer through the support channel listed in the app store listing.
